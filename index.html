What we do

Specialized in stumps.
Trained on whole trees.

From the gnarliest oak stump in your front yard to a full tree removal after a storm — we show up with the right equipment and a clean truck.

Specialty · 01

Stump grinding & removal — to 8″ below grade.

Self-propelled grinders, narrow-gate access (36″), and a finish-rake that leaves your lawn ready for seed or sod. Most residential stumps done in under an hour.

Service · 02

Tree removal

Safe takedowns of dead, damaged, or unwanted trees — close to structures, over fences, near power lines. Crane-assist available.

Service · 03

Trimming & pruning

Crown reduction, deadwooding, structural pruning. ANSI A300 standards — we don't top trees.

Service · 04

Storm cleanup

After the storm: limbs cleared, downed trees cut and hauled, debris removed. Insurance docs on request.

Service · 05

Lot & brush clearing

Clearing for new construction, fence lines, gardens, or property lines. Brush hauled or chipped on-site.
